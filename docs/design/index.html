Where the Game
Comes First

Twin Beach Country Club is a unique 9/18 hole walkable golf course featuring two sets of tees on each hole and three double greens. Nestled in West Bloomfield, our course offers an intimate, unhurried round — the way the game was meant to be played.

The Course

Two Courses
in One

Play nine holes or eighteen — each round is a different experience. Dual tee boxes on every hole and three double greens create variety that rewards repeat play and keeps you coming back.
